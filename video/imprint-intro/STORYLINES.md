# Storylines (30 s product intro, 銘印鑽石)

Picked: **A**. Switch by naming another.

## A — 光，留下來 (built)
- Device: the object is the explanation. Form: match cuts on the diamond.
- First 2 s: footage of a ring on stone, light moving across the facets; 「一段思念，如何成為永恆」 fades in word by word.
- Beats: ring → the question → the lab grower (萃取毛髮、骨灰中的元素 / 於台灣在地培育) → hands holding the cut diamond, 「把最深的情感，銘印成永恆」 fades in → colour flood to brand dark, the mark draws, 銘印鑽石 · 預約制一對一顧問
- Look: the site's own photography as footage, Noto Serif TC, ink type on the light plates, deep mint on 銘印成永恆 only.
- Sound: soft piano and pad, about 70 BPM, swells once under the title.

## B — 每一種羈絆
- Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: object-to-object chain.
- First 2 s: a hand lifts a pendant toward a window; 「每一種羈絆，都有屬於它的光」.
- Beats: pendant → 滿月 / 寵物 / 結髮 / 全家福 moments → title → end card
- Look: cream paper #FBFAF8, ink serif. Sound: acoustic guitar, 80 BPM.

## C — 字裡的光
- Device: type carries the argument. Form: one frame that rebuilds.
- First 2 s: 「把最深的情感」 fades in on cream with diamond footage playing inside the letterforms.
- Beats: words → words → letters open to full-frame footage → end card
- Sound: sparse piano, one note per phrase, opens when the letters open.
